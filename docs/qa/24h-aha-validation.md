# Peel v1 — 24h Double-Aha Validation Plan

This validation script targets Peel's core retention loop: **the 24h Double-Aha model** (PRD § 2). We must verify if the application delivers its primary value propositions within the first 24 hours of use.

## Cohort Selection
- Recruit **5 to 10 active students or junior professionals** who report experiencing "productivity anxiety" or feelings of "failed daily planning."
- Provide them with the deployed H5/web link of Peel.
- Instruct them to use it for **2 full days** to track their daily activities.

---

## 🔑 Validation Milestones

### 1. First Aha (Evening Reflection)
* **Description**: The user completes their day, looks at the Reflection view, and sees their actual spent time visualized as a mosaic block. The copy provides a warm, non-judgmental observation.
* **Question to ask on Day 1 (Night)**: *"When you looked at the evening Reflection summary and time blocks, how did it make you feel about your accomplishments today?"*
  * *Success indicator*: User reports feeling "relieved", "seen", or realizing they were more productive than they estimated (cognitive adjustment).

### 2. Second Aha (Morning Planning)
* **Description**: The user opens the Today view the next morning. They look at the "Morning Advice" heatmap aggregate based on their past 7 days of data, identifying their peak hours to self-plan today's work.
* **Question to ask on Day 2 (Morning)**: *"When you saw the morning peak hour heatmap under Today view, did it influence how you planned or timed your tasks for today?"*
  * *Success indicator*: User reports recognizing their time patterns and using them to proactively schedule their work (expectation calibration).

---

## 📈 Success Criteria
- **Pass condition**: At least **60%** of participants explicitly report that the evening reflection mosaic (Aha B) or the morning heatmap advice (Aha C) felt useful or helped reduce their planning anxiety.
